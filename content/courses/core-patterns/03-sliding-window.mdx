For "the best contiguous stretch" questions, keep a **window** `[left, right]`. Extend `right` one step at a time; when the window breaks the rule, move `left` forward until it is valid again. Each element enters and leaves once: O(n).

```visualizer
sliding-window
```

**Recognise it:** subarrays or substrings, "longest/shortest with at most…", running sums of non-negative values.
